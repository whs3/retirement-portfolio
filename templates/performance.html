Cash Flows

Deposits are positive and withdrawals are negative. Adding, selling, or deleting a holding records a flow for the value that entered or left. Use this form when that value is already in your balances, or to correct an automatic entry. The cards and the monthly table count a flow only after the first day of the selected period, because money already in the starting value is not a gain or a loss.
